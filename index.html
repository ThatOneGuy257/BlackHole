                      PHONE ALERTS (NTFY)

                      1. Install the free ntfy app on your phone.
                      2. In the app, subscribe to this topic on ntfy.sh:

                      Timer alerts say only "session done" or "break over". Deadline alerts include the task's name and group. Your topic works like a password: anyone who knows it can read these alerts. Deadline alerts are scheduled up to 3 days ahead, so open the app every couple of days.

                      SYNC ACROSS DEVICES

                      Your lists are encrypted on this device with your passphrase before they go to GitHub, so GitHub only ever stores scrambled data. Use the same repo, token and passphrase on each device. The passphrase is never saved anywhere; if you forget it, the synced copy can't be recovered (each device keeps its own copy).

                      BACKUP

                      A task with a deadline moves in steadily and crosses the event horizon at its deadline. A task without one crosses 7 days after its last check-in. Tasks are saved in this browser on this device. The page talks to only two places on the internet: ntfy.sh if phone alerts are on, and api.github.com if sync is on.
